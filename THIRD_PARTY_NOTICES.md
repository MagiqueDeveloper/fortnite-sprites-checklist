# Third-party notices and data provenance

Fortnite, its names, characters, and Sprite artwork belong to Epic Games, Inc.
The MIT license does not grant rights to those assets. This is an unofficial fan
project, not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Epic Games.

Treasure Hunters roster, ability summaries, provisional rarities, and image URLs
were checked on 1 November 2026 against the user-selected source:

- https://spritecatch.com/news/treasure-hunters-new-sprites/
- https://spritecatch.com/variants/fortune/
- https://spritecatch.com/variants/cursed/
- Individual family pages linked from that roster, for all 31 image files.

Images are downloaded and centered on transparent 150×150 PNG canvases for offline
use. No replacement images are generated. Rarities are provisional spawn-tier
readings reported by Sprite Catch, not official Epic rarity confirmations. The
launch roster contains ten three-form families and single-form Sweet Dreams.
Gold is excluded until its release is confirmed by the roster source.

Archived Override data/art came from spritechecklist.org, IGN, and Epic artwork.
The old images remain in source for archival purposes. Application dependencies
retain their own licenses, available in their installed packages.

Epic's Fan Content Policy: https://www.epicgames.com/site/en-US/fan-art-policy
